Couch Canvas

Paint by numbers, colouring and drawing, made for a stylus and a slow evening on the sofa. The colours are chosen for you; you just enjoy the painting.

What's inside

  • Paint by numbers. Over 140 pictures for kids and adults, from big simple shapes to long, detailed projects.
  • Colouring and drawing. The same pictures as outlines, or a blank page and all the brushes.
  • Pen first. Pressure-sensitive brushes (pen, chalk, oil, spray), fill and eraser. Fingers move and zoom.
  • Gentle help. Find the next shape, see what you've missed, and change any colour you like.
  • Your own photos. Turn a photo into a paint by numbers or colouring page, right on your tablet.
  • Keep your art. Everything saves as you go. Save finished pictures to Photos or share them.

Nothing but painting

  • No adsNothing pops up while you paint.
  • No accountsOpen it and start. No sign-up, no email.
  • No internet, no trackingThe app works fully offline and collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
