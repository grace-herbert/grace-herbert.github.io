Couch Canvas

Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

In short: Couch Canvas collects no personal information. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you make stays on your tablet.

Who we are

Couch Canvas is made by Brightsparks Digital (Grace Herbert), an independent developer based in Ireland. You can contact us at brightspark.digital.studio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Couch Canvas does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. The app does not request permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or any third-party services, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

What is stored on your tablet

Your paintings, your progress and your settings (such as Day or Night mode) are saved inside the app on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. We cannot see them. They are removed when you delete a painting in the app, or when you uninstall Couch Canvas.

Your own photos

If you choose Use my own picture, Android's photo picker opens and the app can see only the one photo you pick. The photo is turned into a painting entirely on your tablet; it is never uploaded. The app does not keep the original photo file. It keeps only the new picture it draws from it, without the photo's hidden details such as the location where it was taken.

Saving and sharing

Save to Photos saves a copy of your painting to your tablet's Pictures folder (in "Couch Canvas"). Share opens Android's share menu so you can send your painting to an app you choose, such as email or a messaging app. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it, and once shared, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android's own backup on your device, Android may include your Couch Canvas paintings in your device backup so they are restored on a new tablet. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings. We have no access to it.

Children

Couch Canvas includes a Kids section and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads and no chat.

Permissions

Couch Canvas asks for no special permissions. Choosing a photo uses Android's photo picker, which needs no permission and shares only the photo you select.

Your rights

Under data protection law (including the GDPR) you have rights over your personal data. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete, but you are always welcome to contact us with questions. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie).

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. GitHub may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version of the app is released.
